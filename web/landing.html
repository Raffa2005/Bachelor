Double Move Chess

Once per game, move twice.

Each player has one optional Double Move. Activate it before moving, make two legal moves with the same color, then pass the turn.

The rules

How the variant works

The objective and ordinary move rules remain those of chess. The single-use ability changes the structure of one turn.

01 / 03

One ability per side

White and Black each begin with one optional Double Move.

02 / 03

Two legal moves

Activate the ability before the first move. The same side then makes the second move immediately.

03 / 03

Checkmate still wins

Every half-move must be legal. The king cannot be captured, and the objective remains checkmate.
