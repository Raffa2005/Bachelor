Quick guide

Double Strike Chess

  1. Play ordinary chess moves.The board follows normal piece movement, castling, promotion, and en passant rules.
  2. Each side can move twice once.Press “Use double move” before the first move. The ability is spent when that move is accepted.
  3. Complete the second move.The first move may activate the ability only if at least one legal second move will remain. Your clock keeps running until the turn ends.
  4. Need a correction?A takeback returns to your previous decision point and is recorded with the game.
